Send a test question

Open the chat, paste a question and send it. Check the reply against the highlighted catalog values. Replies need the API, worker, database and tunnel from docs/demo.md.

Take over a conversation

  1. Stop the worker with Ctrl+C and let it finish.
  2. Pause the session, then reply from the Crisp inbox.
  3. Resume when Replywork should answer again, then send a new question.
